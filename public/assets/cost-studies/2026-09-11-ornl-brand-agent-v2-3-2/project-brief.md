Here’s the gist for the designer.

This is a redesign for an ORNL story tip graphic about electric drive system technology for heavy-duty EVs. The current graphic exists, but DOE feedback says it needs to do a better job showing why the technology matters: improved reliability, efficiency, smoother operation, less component stress, and longer system life. The big visual shift is that the vehicle should no longer be a passenger car. It needs to read as a Class 6–8 heavy-duty truck.

The core story to preserve is a comparison: the conventional system stays red, and ORNL’s improved version stays green. The system flow still needs to be clear: charger to batteries to inverters to motor. The key innovation is the two inverters working in opposite phase, basically canceling out unwanted electrical effects. Julya is open to a significant rethink if that helps make the concept more intuitive, especially since the caption can carry some of the explanation and the graphic should not lean too heavily on text.
